## Overview

AI and cloud spend is growing faster than your visibility. The Cost Management Agent attributes every dollar, eliminates waste, and enforces budgets automatically. Your agent owns every dollar of cloud and AI spend. Get full visibility into cloud and AI spend as Harness Cost Management Agent attributes, optimizes, and governs at the level of autonomy you choose.
## Key Features

- **Know what your AI actually costs** Spot overpowered models, bloated prompts, and retry loops, then fix the waste automatically before it compounds.
- **Spot overspending before it compounds**, Tie every AI session to a developer, then flag spend that's outpacing the code they're actually shipping.
- **Catch cloud waste before it adds up** See cost anomalies the moment they happen, broken down by provider, so nothing slips by unnoticed.
