I’ve been experimenting with simple AI-based classification for my Gmail inbox.

The original idea was fairly simple: instead of asking an LLM to read every email and figure out what to do with it, could I use a smaller, decision-oriented model for some of these tasks?

That led me to Laya.

What started as a small Gmail experiment eventually turned into something more interesting: establishing a baseline, building a labeled dataset, fine-tuning a model locally, comparing it with a remote JEV model, and evaluating the results using Harness Evals.

Here’s what I tried, what surprised me, and what I learned along the way.
